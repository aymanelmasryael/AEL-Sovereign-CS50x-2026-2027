About

Engineering with intention

I am a software architect and full-stack engineer who believes great software is equal parts structure and craft. My work spans the entire lifecycle of a product — from shaping system architecture and data models, to writing the front-end experiences users interact with.

My approach is grounded in clean engineering principles: clear abstractions, well-defined interfaces, and code that reads as thoughtfully as it runs. I care deeply about accessibility, performance, and the small details that turn a working product into a delightful one.

Away from the keyboard, I mentor emerging developers and contribute to open source, always looking for ways to make software more approachable and robust for everyone who relies on it.
